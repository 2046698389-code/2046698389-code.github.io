The three things that break today

1. You don't collect it

Most stores create a fake "Deposit" product and hope the customer adds it. Most customers don't — and in a DRS market that's a compliance gap, not just lost revenue.

2. You can't refund it

A customer returns 4 of 6 bottles two weeks later. You need to refund the deposit only, by quantity, against the original order. Order refunds on a fake product line corrupt stock and reports.

3. The month doesn't close

Collected, refunded, still outstanding — three numbers your accountant and your scheme operator will ask for. Without a ledger you're adding up orders by hand.

What this fixes

Deposit added automatically per container, kept out of your product price, refunded per bottle, and exported as a ledger your accountant can read.

Questions

Do I need Shopify Plus to charge a bottle deposit?

No. The deposit is added as a cart line and carried through to the order, which works on Basic, Shopify and Advanced. Custom checkout UI requires Plus — you don't need it to charge and refund a deposit.

Is a bottle deposit taxable?

In most deposit return markets a refundable deposit is not treated as taxable revenue when it is stated separately and refunded on return. Confirm with your accountant — the app lets you mark the deposit line as non-taxable.

How do I refund a deposit without refunding the product?

Refund the deposit line only, with quantity equal to the bottles returned. The product line stays untouched, and the return is recorded against the original order so your month-end balance stays correct.
